# Getting help

1. **Check the [FAQ](docs/GUIDE.md#faq)** in the user guide. It covers the blink when
   plugging in, Fn taps, double popups, color modes with HDR and more.
2. **Search the [issues](../../issues)**: someone may have had the same problem.
3. **Open a new issue** with the Bug report form. Paste the output of
   **System > Copy diagnostics** in the app: it holds the versions, what the app found on
   your laptop and the last log lines, never the serial number or your user name.

The full log is in `C:\Program Files\Art14Helper\Logs\Art14Helper.log` (System > Open log).

Art14 Helper is a free, unofficial project maintained in spare time, so answers may take a
few days. HONOR doesn't provide support for it.
