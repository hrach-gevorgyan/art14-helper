# Code of conduct

Art14 Helper is a small community project. Everyone who takes part, in issues, pull
requests, discussions or anywhere else in the project, is expected to help keep it a
welcoming place.

## Expected

- Be respectful and patient, especially with newcomers and people writing in a second
  language.
- Assume good intent, and give and take feedback about the work, not the person.
- Stay on topic and keep reports and reviews constructive.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or deliberately derailing discussions.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their permission.
- Sexualized language or imagery.

## Enforcement

The maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues or pull requests, and block
people who break these rules, in proportion to what happened. To report a problem
privately, use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment or account, or open a
private security advisory if it involves a safety issue (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)).
Reports are handled confidentially.

This code of conduct applies within the project's spaces and when someone represents the
project in public.
